# City Bank ELDP — Demo Pitch Script

**For:** the RiseUp Labs presenter at the City Bank PLC tender evaluation
**Length:** about 20 minutes plus questions
**Setup:**
- Open http://localhost:5174 full screen (1920×1080 is best; 1366×768 works).
- A fresh browser always starts at Step 1. If you've rehearsed in this browser, click **Continue with City Bank SSO**; signing in starts fresh.
- Turn on **More → Presenter mode** for larger text. It also shows **Do this step for me** beside the red step button.

The words to say are in plain text. *Click cues are in italics.*

**How to drive it**
- **The step bar:** one line under the menu. Left: who you're viewing as. Middle: the training record. Right: the step chip ("● Step n/24 · title"; click it for the Guided journey), the red button for the next action, and the ··· menu.
- **Hand-offs:** when a step passes to another person, the demo shows "Sending to …" and opens that person's screen. A green arrival line says what was received.
- **Dashboard stops:** at some steps a **Show: … dashboard** link appears (in the step note, the ··· menu and the Guided journey). It opens that role's dashboard without changing anything; **Back to the current step** returns you. Want every hand-off to land on the dashboard first? Turn on **More → Hand-offs open the role dashboard first**.
- **Getting back on track:**
  - **Going back:** **··· → Previous step** reopens the last finished step. The step chip opens the Guided journey, which lists every step; open any finished one, or restart from it.
  - **Getting lost:** the side menu always shows where you are, and **··· → Overview** shows the whole picture.

## 1. Opening (1 minute)

*Start on the welcome screen.*

Good morning, and thank you for having us.

We'll follow one real piece of work through the whole platform: Corporate Banking needs its credit officers to improve in credit analysis. You'll see that need become a training, then attendance, assessment and learning, and finally a change in capability that management can see. Every person who touches it appears in their own role. The people, IDs and amounts are fictional demonstration data.

*Click **Continue with City Bank SSO**.*

Sign-in uses City Bank's identity system. We're signed in as Farzana Haque, Head of Corporate Banking and the division's Learning Champion.

## 2. Home (1 minute)

*On Home. Point to the learning need (18 of 20 officers, level 2 against target 4), then the AI recommendation beside it. Click **Why was this recommended?**, then close it.*

Corporate Banking has a Credit Analysis capability gap. The system recommends Advanced Credit Risk Management and shows why: skill gap, role requirement and mandatory learning rules. AI-assisted, human decision required: the system suggests, Farzana decides.

*Point to "What happens next", the training journey, then Recent activity and Enterprise pulse.*

This journey is an example flow through one training. Each module, such as exams, feedback or the library, also works on its own.

*Click **Review recommendation** to open the training needs page. (Short on time: the red **Raise training request** on Home goes straight to the request, filled in.)*

## 3. From divisional requirement to training (8 minutes)

### Steps 1–2: Training Needs Assessment, then the training request (FR 15, 186, 14, 49–64)
*Lands on **Training needs for Corporate Banking**. Show the three paths: competency gap; **Review survey output** → **Use as supporting evidence**; **Review requests** → tick two → **Combine 2 selected as supporting evidence**. In **Training requirement summary**, click **Raise training request**.*

A requirement can come from the competency gap, a survey or nomination round, or employees' development requests. Any one is enough, and everything chosen travels with the request.

*On the request form the title and justification are already filled in; **Save & Next**. On the Trainer tab, show **Add More** and the search by EID. Then **Save & Next**. On the Nominations tab, click **Add all** on the suggested officers, then **Submit to HR**.*

The competency data showed 18 of Farzana's 20 officers below target in credit analysis, which is the divisional requirement. She raises the request on the workbook's tabs: Program, Trainer and Nominations. Mode and Medium are the workbook's own lists. She can **Save & Exit** a draft at any time.

*The demo hands off to HR.*

Notice we're now Nusrat in HR, and the green line says what just arrived. That happens at every hand-off.

### Step 3: HR reviews the request (FR 65–66)
*Point to the three groups of facts, then the decision panel.*

*Optional dashboard stop: **Show: HR operations dashboard** — the new request in HR's work for today. Then **Back to the current step**.*

HR sees the request organised into three groups, plus what ELDP already knows. The workbook gives HR choices here: **Schedule Training** directly, **Forward** to another HR Admin, or collect nominations first. We'll collect nominations.

*Click **Create Nomination Request**.*

### Step 4: Training Nomination Request Setup (FR 67–87)
*Step through **Program → Trainer → Nomination**. On Nomination, point to the recipients table and the email preview. Click **Save & Send Nomination Request**.*

Three clear steps, matching the workbook. One editable email goes to every Learning Champion with a nominate link.

### Step 5: The Learning Champion nominates (FR 88)
*Filter by Division, then click **Submit 35 nominations** in the bar under the table.*

### Steps 6–8: Conflicts, HR decisions and finalising (FR 89–91, 114)
*On Nomination Status, open **Tanvir Hasan** to show what HRIS, leave and Outlook say. Close it. Filter Eligibility to "Conflict", **select all**, then **Remove** with a reason. Keep Tanvir with a reason. Click **Finalize participants**.*

The system checked every nominee. HR decides, one at a time or in bulk, and every decision needs a reason that goes into the audit trail. Undo is available. Finalising locks the participant list.

### Step 9: Training Scheduling (FR 92–131)
*Show **Load Training Profile: Yes**, the venue clash and **Use Hall A**. Step through Trainer, Participants (**Save List & Next**), Notification, Feedback and Undertaking (**Training Bond**). On Review & submit, click **Save & send budget to Finance**.*

Scheduling loads the nomination profile automatically; that's the workbook's Load Training Profile. Each tab uses the workbook's own buttons.

### Step 10: Finance approves the budget (proposed workflow)
*Lands on Finance. Optional: **Show: Finance dashboard (proposed)** — the budget waiting in the queue. Back on the step, click **Approve budget**.*

This step is our proposal. The workbook asks only for the budget fields, but a second approver protects the bank.

### Step 11: Invitations (FR 116–121)
*Lands back on HR, on the Notification tab. Click **Send Training Invitation Now**.*

## 5. Training day, assessment and records (4 minutes)

### Step 17: Attendance (FR 3, 137–143)
*Switch between **Day 1** and **Day 2**. Click **Sync Teams attendance**. Filter to Unmarked and bulk-mark Present. Mark one person Absent individually. Click **Submit & Update Training Database**.*

One register per day. Employees self-mark, Teams fills in online attendance, and HR controls the rest, one by one or in bulk. Submit stays disabled until everyone is marked.

*Dashboard stop after submitting: **Show: Supervisor team dashboard** — who attended, and who missed which day.*

### Steps 18–20: Feedback, closing and records
*Give feedback as Ayesha. As HR, open **Planned Training**, enter the approval and payment numbers, and **Complete training**. The demo shows Ayesha's updated record.*

### Step 21: Connected e-learning (FR 190–198)
*Open the course overview, then play a little of the video and open the PDF and the scenario. Finish the checkpoint and show the certificate with its QR code.*

### Step 22: Post-training assessment (FR 210–217)
*As the trainer: **Clear** integrity, then **Accept** the AI-suggested score, then **Release result to candidate**. Open **View all results**.*

Every attendee took an assessment for this training. AI observes and suggests, and the trainer decides. The results table shows who passed and who failed, and HR assigns re-attempts in one click.

*Dashboard stops after releasing: **Show: Supervisor team dashboard** (who passed and failed in Mahmud's team), then **Show: Corporate Banking training needs** (the learning need and department progress).*

## Presenter safety net
- **Lost?** Use the side menu, **··· → Overview**, or the step chip (Guided journey). The red button always opens the next step.
- **On a dashboard stop?** **Back to the current step** in the blue banner.
- **Need to go back?** **··· → Previous step**, or **Guided journey → Revisit this step** / **Restart from here**.
- **Short on time?** **Do this step for me**, in the ··· menu or visible in Presenter mode.
- **Bulk action went wrong?** Click **Undo** in the message at the bottom.
- **Start over?** **More → Reset demo**, or sign in again.
- **"Is that integration live?"** No. It's labelled simulated, and built as adapters in delivery.
